Mountain weather changes faster than any forecast. The Ridge shell weighs about as much as a sandwich, packs into its own pocket, and lives in your pack until the clouds roll in.

The hood adjusts with one hand, and the pit zips let heat out on the climb, so you're not choosing between wet from rain and wet from sweat.
