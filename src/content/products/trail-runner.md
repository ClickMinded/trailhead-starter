The shoe we hand to people walking into the shop for their first real hike. It's light enough that you forget you're wearing it, and the lugs bite into wet rock and loose dirt without feeling clumsy on pavement.

No break-in drama: wear them around the house for a few evenings, then take them out for a short walk before you commit to a long one. Our [guide to breaking in trail shoes](/journal/break-in-trail-shoes/) walks through it.
