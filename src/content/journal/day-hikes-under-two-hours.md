The best first hike is shorter than you think. Two to three hours on the trail, a little climbing, and a view or a lake at the turnaround point.

## What to look for

- **Under 8 km round trip.** You can always do a longer one next weekend.
- **Less than 300 m of climbing.** Enough to feel it, not enough to ruin your evening.
- **A clear payoff.** A lake, a summit, or a viewpoint gives you a natural place to eat lunch and turn around.
- **A well-marked trail.** Look for painted blazes on trees, the same ones we use as our logo.

## Before you go

Check the weather and the trail conditions the morning of the hike, tell someone where you're going, and pack a headlamp even if you plan to be back by lunch.
