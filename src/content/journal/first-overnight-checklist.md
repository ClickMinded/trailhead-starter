Your first overnight trip is mostly about one thing: being warm, dry and fed when the sun goes down. Everything on this list serves one of those three.

## Shelter and sleep

- A tent you've set up at least once in your backyard
- A sleeping bag rated a little colder than the forecast low
- A sleeping pad. It keeps you warm as much as it keeps you comfortable.

## Warm and dry

- A rain shell, even if the forecast is clear
- One warm layer you only wear at camp
- Dry socks, sealed in a bag

## Fed

- Dinner, breakfast, and more snacks than you think you need
- A stove, fuel, a lighter, and a mug you can heat water in
- Water treatment, unless you're carrying all your water

## Leave at home

The second pair of shoes, the camp chair, and the hatchet. You'll want them for about ten minutes and carry them for two days.
